    Item and Group Level Inter-Annotator Agreement Analysis

    This notebook extends the overall IAA analysis with detailed item-level and group-level investigations.

    Additional Analysis Features

    • Item-Level Agreement: Cohen's Kappa and agreement rates for individual checklist items
    • Group-Level Agreement: Aggregated metrics for checklist sections (e.g., "Abstract", "Methods")
    • Problematic Item Detection: Identification of items with poor agreement
    • Rating Pattern Analysis: Distribution of Yes/No/NA responses by item and group
    • Comparative Visualizations: Side-by-side analysis of different agreement levels
